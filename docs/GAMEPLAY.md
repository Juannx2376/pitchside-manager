# Gameplay guide

A tour of the systems from the manager's point of view.

## The loop

Press **Continue** to advance day by day. The game stops for things that need you: your match, a press
conference, an incoming bid, a job offer, the end of the season and **Deadline Day**. Missions, weekly training
events and tasks appear in the **task bar** on the Home screen.

Navigation has six sections (top bar): **Home**, **Squad** (squad / tactics / training), **Transfers**
(transfers / scouting / Deadline Day), **Club** (finances / staff), **Competitions** (tables, calendar, world)
and **Career** (career, settings, help). Keyboard: `1–6` jump to a section, `Q` / `E` move between tabs,
`Ctrl+Space` continues.

## Tactics

- Pick a formation or a template, then fine-tune every player: **role**, **focus**, **instructions**,
  **attacking channel** and **passing risk**.
- Team plan: build-up style, defensive approach, width, depth, marking, transition, corner and free-kick styles.
- **Leading / trailing plans** switch your approach from minute 55; each side has one **timeout** per half.
- The tactics tab shows a *tactical profile* (possession, chance quality, fatigue, …) and how well your XI fits
  the style. Player familiarity with roles grows with tactical sessions and matches.

## Players

| Concept | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Rating (OVR) | Base rating plus a **dynamic** value for today (form, morale, fitness). Cards show both. |
| Potential | Exact only for your own players; `x–y` after scouting; hidden otherwise. |
| Growth profile | Six profiles (early bloomer, late bloomer, steady, explosive, short peak, longevity). |
| Squad role | key / first / rotation / backup / youth — mismatches hurt happiness and trigger promises. |
| Growth plans | Pick a development goal; milestones and a training bonus follow. |

## Transfers

1. **Scout.** Order a player analysis; it takes days and returns *estimates* with an error margin.
2. **Enquire.** The club and the player reply in 1–3 days. Stars may be untouchable.
3. **Negotiate in parallel.** Make a club bid (fee, instalments, bonus, sell-on %, swap players, entry date)
   and a contract offer (wage, years, role, bonuses, clauses, agent fee). Tension rises if you push too hard.
4. **Agreement → presentation.** Rivals may reopen the deal for stars. The reaction of fans, press and board
   is revealed only after the signing.

Selling: list players, receive offers (AI clubs bid for listed players), **negotiate counter-offers** (hidden
ceiling, 3 rounds), accept swaps, or sell with a **buy-back clause** and re-sign the player later.

Money: the **Your money** panel shows the available budget, committed amounts, instalments, expected sales and
what carries over to next season. If a bid does not fit, the assistant proposes players to list, moving money
from wages, or paying in instalments. Every sale returns **80 %** of the fee to the transfer budget.

### Deadline Day

On the last day of a window the market runs hour by hour. Replies arrive in 1–3 hours, rival managers
raise prices or snipe your targets, and **Close now** pays a premium (+12 % fee, +10 % wage) to settle a deal
immediately. At midnight everything unresolved closes and you get a summary.

## Training and academy

- Assign a coach to each group, choose a session focus and intensity. The weekly gain is deliberately slow and
  depends on coach quality, facilities, age, professionalism and remaining potential.
- Choose a **pre-season camp** once per season (fitness / tactical / technical) in the first 28 days.
- "Ease off before matches" lowers intensity when a match is within two days.
- The **Progress** tab charts who is improving or declining.
- Academy: capacity depends on facilities; pick a **youth policy** (balanced / high potential / ready sooner);
  each new season you sign prospects within your free places, can **loan** them out for minutes and promote
  those marked *Ready*.

## Finances

Season budgets come from cash and projected revenue, with FPF-style rules per confederation. The ledger tracks
TV, sponsors, matchday, prize money, sales, wages and more; you can take loans, build facilities, expand the
stadium, set ticket prices and sign sponsors. Overspending can trigger sanctions and a transfer embargo.

## Career

Board confidence, season objectives and weekly missions feed **manager XP**. Level up to spend points in the
**perks tree** and claim rewards on the season **path**. If you are sacked you are *between jobs*: apply for
clubs and wait for offers — the game never ends.
